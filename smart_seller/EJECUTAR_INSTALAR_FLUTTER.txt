========================================
  CÓMO EJECUTAR instalar_flutter.ps1
========================================

En PowerShell COMO ADMINISTRADOR, ejecuta UNO de estos comandos:

OPCIÓN 1 (recomendada - ruta completa):
----------------------------------------
Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Force
& "D:\losoft\DEMOV2\smart_seller\instalar_flutter.ps1"

OPCIÓN 2 (si ya estás en la carpeta del proyecto):
----------------------------------------
Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Force
cd D:\losoft\DEMOV2\smart_seller
& ".\instalar_flutter.ps1"

OPCIÓN 3 (invocar con -File):
----------------------------------------
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "D:\losoft\DEMOV2\smart_seller\instalar_flutter.ps1"
